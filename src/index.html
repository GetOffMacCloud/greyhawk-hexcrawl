Where your work lives

In this browser, on this machine, at this address — nowhere else. Clearing site data erases it. Switch on Autosave… to keep a live copy in a real file, and use Export for a snapshot you can move somewhere safe.

Notes from players

Paste what a player sent you. Approving adds the note to your map and marks it shared, so it appears the next time you export the player map. Denying discards it here — their own copy is untouched.
